# Manager and director defaults — 0.32.0

Hiring an available manager delegates daily operations without another configuration step. Appointing a director adds a growth objective and strategic authority. Promoting the local manager creates a managing director with both responsibilities, the existing responsibility raise, one employment record and one payroll cost. Explicit saved overrides remain effective.

## Behavior

- Managers handle existing hiring, scheduling, prices, purchasing, projects, license renewals, annual pay reviews, eligible leave, property care and supported operating decisions. Training now has a default $500 monthly allowance; default training waits for another available employee covering the same role. Optional support roles can be reduced to an explicitly configured staff target. Existing service coverage can be renewed through real queued work; creating a department alone grants no benefit.
- Collections follow free reminders, installment proposals, then a contingency agency. After ordinary attempts are exhausted, balances above twice the outside legal cost can receive one legal collection attempt. A qualified owned legal department with room in its queue is preferred; otherwise outside counsel reserves $1,800 for 20 hours. Real time, payroll, outside costs and uncertain recoveries remain modeled. Overloaded internal teams do not receive unlimited work.
- An exhausted balance up to $1,000 can be written off automatically. Larger losses, prohibited actions, unavailable funds and commitments above authority create a named manager question in Decision inbox. Write-offs are bad-debt expense, never cash received. Managers do not repeatedly retry the same unsuccessful recovery method.
- Routine invoices awaiting review are identified as assigned to management. The player can still choose a recovery option directly. Required approvals appear in the inbox, completed decisions in Leadership activity, and approved actions recheck current conditions. Long skips stop before unauthorized commitments; routine review still requires an available employee.
- A director's default growth policy permits equipment investment in supported industries after positive recorded profit, 14 operating days and at least 85% use of observed capacity. Engineering, construction and trades target 120% equipment capacity; other supported operations target 160%. Staff and demand continue to constrain output. Equipment work costs real cash and takes 21 days.
- At 160% capacity a growth director can fund a second same-industry location when recorded demand, profit, authority and reserves permit it. Setup costs and working capital are funded separately. The new location keeps its startup delay, hiring requirements and shared legal obligations; it inherits eligible director oversight. The default location limit is two. This is a bounded same-industry expansion routine, not unrestricted acquisitions.
- Managers remain responsible for local daily work when a separate director is available. Director fallback, employment schedules, leave, qualifications and workload limits remain in force. Default subordinate authority now automatically applies every explicit parent restriction and charges the shared parent budget; a separate manager contract is optional.

## Financial defaults and overrides

Existing industry-sized manager purchase ceilings and the $6,000 monthly salary ceiling remain. The default contingency collection-fee ceiling is $25,000 because fees are deducted from recovered cash. A saved purchase ceiling overrides that default unless a separate collection ceiling is chosen. The full contingent fee counts against transaction and cumulative authority even though no money is paid up front. Default agency collection can proceed during a cash shortage; explicitly configured cash restrictions still apply.

A newly appointed director's daily shared commitment limit is the greatest of $100,000, four ordinary purchase ceilings, and one standard opening budget for the business's industry (including working capital). For general retail this is $200,000. Monthly cumulative authority is four times the assigned limit. Existing appointments keep their chosen limits. Scope, county, headcount, compensation and project-value limits remain enforced, and new payroll commitments count for the full year. Default cash checks preserve seven days of known obligations and configured reserves without assuming customer collections. Financing, property acquisition and asset sales still require explicit authority or player approval.

The business's Leadership section now offers **Promote manager to managing director**, **Use default manager policies** and, when a director exists, **Use default director growth**. Restoring defaults removes corresponding routine overrides but preserves authority contracts, parent restrictions, cash reserves, accumulated spending and employee records. It does not spend money or approve an existing question. Partial policy edits retain only the submitted choices so that changing pricing or hiring does not silently turn off later director growth.

## Persistence and validation

Defaults are derived without writing to a save when a page is opened. Existing saved overrides, IDs, ledger entries and ownership relationships are preserved; no live campaign migration is required. Regression coverage includes collections, genuine legal delivery, small write-offs, parent limits, promotion pay/history, growth cash flows, location ownership reconciliation, explicit overrides, read-only previews, seeded daily/batch equivalence and save/load consistency.

## Limits and next step

Daily work uses the game's bounded routines. Hiring and price reviews run weekly; shift alignment retains the existing 08:00 convention and does not redesign schedules for every industry. Banks, property-only operations and specialist developments still use their dedicated workflows rather than generic equipment growth. New financing, asset purchases, flipping and other exceptional commitments need authority; appointing a director does not create funds or guarantee expansion. Existing explicit disabled or zero-limit policies remain until the player changes or restores them. Existing open approvals remain open until reviewed or deferred.

Restart **Play Empire Manager.cmd** to load this release. Next improvements can refine industry-specific staffing and shift planning and add more growth proposal types while retaining the same authority and financial checks.
