# Home-office leadership transfer — 0.33.0

Open an active employee and use **Move to home-office leadership**. Select an operating home-office company, Home Office Director / Division VP / VP of Corporate Services, and one primary supervisor (the player is the default). The normal preview shows the responsibility raise, all overseen businesses, employer payroll changes and home-office cash. Confirmation moves the existing employee to a new position and leaves the former location's position available for replacement hiring. Existing local managers retain priority for daily business work; the promoted leader provides strategic oversight and the existing fallback when a manager is unavailable.

This is a real employment change with the same person and employment IDs, schedule, tenure, earned leave, approved leave dates, individual benefit agreements and annual-review timing. Future payroll belongs to the home office; historical expense and unpaid wages remain at the original employer and retain their named financial drilldown. The new employer's benefit policies apply prospectively. The destination must cover known obligations and 30 days of payroll without assuming customer receipts. Invalid funding, scope, employment, role, ownership or a reporting cycle causes no partial transfer.

Director oversight is promoted in the original scope/authority record. Existing direct roots and inherited subsidiaries remain covered, with the selected home office added. Saved contract identifiers, parent contracts, current daily spending and monthly audit charges remain unchanged. The promotion does not refresh a budget or relax an explicit contract. An explicit contract may need its organizational/geographic scope updated before the leader can spend for the home office. Capacity is counted once, at 30 minutes per covered business per working day with a maximum of eight, plus three hours reserved for the primary role.

Home Office Directors and Corporate Services VPs can prioritize real queued service tasks according to their objective and purchasing scope; changes appear in leadership activity. Delivery still needs qualified staff and available time. Executives can allocate approved capital only from their actual employing-parent account to eligible directly owned businesses, with real paired investment entries, cumulative commitments, current reserves and the same daily budget. An existing resource-allocation argument mismatch was corrected and covered by a real funding regression test. Division VPs retain business oversight and these bounded allocations.

Career history, current employee duties, management overview, organization charts and executive policy controls show the promotion. Changing designation within the same office replaces the previous executive position, rather than leaving a duplicate vacancy. Equal compensated responsibility does not stack a raise. Existing individual appointments and single-business play continue to work; this feature creates no mandatory headquarters.

Limits: the destination must already be an operating office. This workflow handles managers/directors; legacy separate executive appointments still use their existing appointment controls. Active shared-service or named department staffing must be detached first to avoid silently widening a service team's staff selection. Explicit conflicting director appointments must be resolved before promotion. A location's vacant manager position is not filled instantly; hire a replacement or let authorized director hiring run. General transfers to arbitrary operating jobs are outside this increment.
