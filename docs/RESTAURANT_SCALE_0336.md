# Restaurant growth and staffing — 0.33.6

Open an owned restaurant's business page. Save **Opening hours, demand and staffing plan** to enable meal-time operations. Existing restaurants retain their previous model until this explicit step; existing employees, balances, leave and history carry over. No headquarters is required.

## Implemented behavior

- A restaurant can open on any selected weekdays, including weekends, for 4–16 hours. Daily demand varies with the county economy, competition, customer relationships, pricing and seeded uncertainty. Customers arrive in weighted breakfast, lunch and dinner periods; unused morning labor cannot satisfy evening demand.
- Meals consume simultaneous chef and server minutes, ingredients and physical kitchen/dining capacity. Prep cooks, dishwashers and hosts use their own finite time to remove preparation, washing and hosting work from chefs/servers. Managers provide hourly supervision. Equipment condition, time off, training, shared assignments and service work affect capacity through the existing employee time budgets.
- Four single-location layouts provide 40/80/140/220 seats and 35/75/125/200 kitchen meals per hour before equipment and staffing constraints. The successive upgrades cost $65,000/$140,000/$240,000, take 14/21/28 calendar days and reduce kitchen capacity by 25% during work. Full payment enters construction in progress, then equipment on completion; incremental operating costs begin then. Future costs enter cash forecasts. Capacity does not create customers or guarantee profit.
- Successful service develops a bounded local audience. Paid advertising can accelerate that development. Unaffordable advertising is reduced to the cash actually spent. Ingredient replenishment follows forecast demand and a selected 1–5 day stock target, with the existing funding, payroll reserve and delegated purchasing limits. Existing ingredient spoilage remains an expense.
- The staffing guide calculates role hours and full-time equivalents from forecast or a player-selected planning volume. That planning number does not generate sales. The seven-day forecast uses cloned simulation state and actual schedules/leave/shared commitments. It shows meals service can cover, assuming ingredients are available; actual trade can be lower.
- **Grow toward restaurant workload** chooses the largest role-hours shortage when there is no open vacancy, while retaining the existing one-attempt-per-Monday hiring cadence. Active and joining employees count toward the chosen headcount ceiling (up to 100). Annual payroll commitments, compensation bands, cash reserves, parent scope and explicit restrictions still apply. A manager does not fill all 50 positions simply because 50 are allowed.
- Managers use a deterministic staggered weekly rota and reconsider it when the active team changes or a new plan is saved. The player can review balanced shifts immediately, edit individual working days/start times, or pin schedules. Weekly contracted hours, pay, stable identities, leave and reporting relationships are preserved. Cross-employment overlaps are rejected; shared-service staff and leaders with reserved oversight stay on their existing schedule. Explicit authority restrictions create normal management requests and stop long skips before unapproved changes.
- The business page has restaurant controls, cash and employer-cost totals, a searchable/filterable weekly roster with a bounded scrolling region, seven-day coverage and the latest hour-by-hour operating result. Schedule changes use the normal preview/confirm/save process. Employee pages now display their actual working weekdays.

## Limits and next step

Schedules are recurring weekly patterns with one continuous shift per employee per day. The planner is a deterministic coverage heuristic, not a global optimizer; split shifts, individually placed meal breaks and dated one-off shift swaps are not modeled. Small shared assignments are respected but not rescheduled automatically. Forecasts hold skills and commitments fixed and are not guaranteed sales.

Physical restaurant expansions are initiated by the player in this increment. Existing director/equipment-growth behavior remains, but autonomous construction of these larger restaurant layouts is not added. Growth layouts represent funded fit-out capacity, not parcel zoning or a separate property development approval process. Other industries retain their existing operating models. The next step is to reuse the volume/shift planning structure for groceries and factories, then add dated rota overrides if needed.

Source installation uses a manifest, checksum comparison, backup and rollback. Save files are not installed or modified. Restart the running game after updating so Python, templates and assets all use 0.33.6.
