# Financial detail — 0.17.0

Open **Finances** for an account or choose **Include owned companies**. Assets and obligations are now expandable sections with supporting rows and direct links. Section shortcuts lead to cash, property, equipment and payroll. This is a read-only reporting extension; it creates no fictional asset inventory, payroll expense, cash transfer or account migration.

- **Cash by account:** names the legal entity holding each recorded cash balance. Individual bank accounts/physical locations are not modeled. A bank's cash is labeled liquidity backing deposit obligations, rather than free acquisition capital. Separate deposits, escrow and reserve ledger accounts remain separate balances.
- **Owned properties:** names, owner, location, category, use/occupancy, condition, acquisition date and recorded cost. Estimated value appears in its own column. Links open the actual property with its owner context. The list uses property ownership, not tenancy.
- **Equipment:** complete capitalized addition/disposal history from the journal, including opening equipment, headquarters fit-out and expansions. It is not truncated to the journal's latest 100 entries. Accumulated depreciation shows gross cost, net book value, physical condition when recorded and expandable dated depreciation entries. The current simulation records purchase pools, not individually priced machines. Expensed local improvements/maintenance are not presented as capital assets.
- **Payroll still owed:** maps saved outstanding obligation lots to stable employee IDs, their employer and earliest due date. It follows the simulation's existing oldest-due payment allocation, including partial settlement. Missing historical attribution is explicitly unassigned.
- **Payroll breakdown:** current-month journal expenses per employee for wages, benefits, variable incentives/bonuses, severance and employer payroll tax. Current unpaid payroll is a separate column including earlier months. Former employees remain visible when their expenses or obligations exist. Payroll tax is a separate payable; it is not added to the wages/benefits liability. Shared employees are counted under their employer once.
- **Current monthly pay structure:** base salary, scheduled overtime, shift premium, benefit cost, estimated employer tax and total monthly run rate for each active/joining employee. Active and joining totals are separated. These are prospective current contract rates, not fabricated past expense or guaranteed incentive costs.
- **Other supporting balances:** customer invoices and due/default status, unbilled work by concurrent contract, property lease deposits and dated liabilities. Any amount without supporting records remains a clearly labeled ledger balance, so detail sums match the shown balance without guessed attribution.

Existing finance explanatory text now reflects implemented income tax, financing and depreciation. Recorded expense includes tax; the profit metric is labeled accordingly. The original transaction journal remains available.

Known limits: equipment remains pooled, the cash ledger does not identify a banking provider, and some old bonuses/opening payroll balances lack an employee ID. Such records are not retrospectively assigned to guessed equipment or people. Current monthly employer tax estimates can differ from actual daily rounding. Reports show current entity scope; they do not introduce a new dated ownership/consolidation model.
