# Operations release 0.11.0

This extends the existing game and preserves the previous handoff, working features and custom comments. Money remains integer cents, commands use the existing atomic preview/commit boundary, and each simulated day is persisted. The implementation was staged and tested outside the live campaign directory.

## Financial interpretation

- Deposits belong to tenants or bank customers and remain liabilities. Bank deposits do not create capital. Lending is limited by liquidity and a capital-to-exposure ceiling.
- Accepted future work is a commitment before it becomes an expense. Prepaid outside services, construction work and flip reserves remain assets until consumed or released. Known internal labor rates and a cushion inform commitment forecasts.
- Internal rent/services/interest/support do not create consolidated income. Capitalized internal construction offsets the actual provider payroll cost rather than booking a markup as group profit.
- Factory orders already accepted in older saves retain their progress, unbilled earnings and identities. Production, shipment and collection are distinct.
- Property results distinguish rent collected, operating income, actual tracked payments, deposits, estimated value, debt and sale proceeds. Historical tracking begins when the new subsystem observes an asset; it does not fabricate earlier detail. Income tax remains an entity-level expense. Secured-property interest is tracked separately.
- Flip profit deducts acquisition basis, capitalized renovation, holding/repair/interest/inspection costs and selling costs. Transferred receivables are excluded from property-sale profit. Debt repayment and deposit transfers affect cash returned, while unused project reserves are returned separately.
- Personal or holding-parent insolvency ends further time advancement. A subsidiary closure does not automatically bankrupt an unrelated parent. A company's operating locations share legal obligations; separate subsidiaries do not acquire that relationship implicitly.

## Persistence and compatibility

Opening an older supported campaign creates `campaign.before-operations-<id>.sqlite3` before updating its subsystem version to 2. The upgrade retains existing account balances, employee/position IDs, ownership and simulation date and adds missing office/mixed-use market opportunities. Older source releases reject this newer subsystem version rather than silently dropping obligations. Restore the matching campaign backup when rolling back source.

The installer backs up source and copies a reviewed file manifest. It does not copy test campaigns or replace live save files. An already-running game needs to be closed and reopened to load the release. Its existing revision checks protect against stale writes.

## Practical limits and explicit later work

This is a playable simulation model, not a claim that every aspirational item in the historical handoff is production complete. Department work products cover the operating workflows above; bespoke litigation, exhaustive HR succession planning and detailed departmental project types are not separate full simulations. Specialist property-service companies use the existing operating industries and contractor path; separate hotel/parking/security catalogs are not added in this release.

Land assembly, feasibility, district approvals, phased district construction, preleasing and district improvements remain explicit later development, as allowed by the design. Legacy whole-building renovation and sale commands remain for compatibility alongside the more detailed workbench; they do not have all of its conditional-offer stages. No new clean-machine installer qualification, decades-long balance claim or large-workforce performance claim is made.

Next development gate: extend specialist service/property industry catalogs and deeper departmental objectives, then district development. Playtest longer portfolios and large organizations before broadening autonomous investment choices. See VALIDATION.md for tests actually executed.
