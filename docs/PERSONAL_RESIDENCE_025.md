# Player personal residence — 0.25.0

Open a personally owned residential property and choose **Make this my residence**, or use **Personal finances → Personal residence** to choose an eligible home. Changes use the normal decision review, autosave and event history. Property cards and the property header identify the player's residence; the personal page links directly to it.

The player has one residence. A move validates the new home before releasing the old one; a successful switch leaves the former home vacant. **Move out** returns the current residence to vacant rental/investment use without automatically finding tenants or selling it. Designation and moving out have no invented cash charge or income.

Eligibility: personal-portfolio ownership, residential use, vacant and undivided, at least 40 overall condition, no existing occupants or active lease, no pending sale, no unresolved funded flip and no unfinished property work. An occupied rental must complete its lease workflow first. Company-owned housing and living in one unit of a divided rental building are outside this increment. Buying property is still a separate action.

Residence uses the existing persisted property occupancy fields: occupied status, personal_residence use and personal occupant. There is no additional owner identity or duplicate property record. Validation enforces the single-residence and lease invariants. Existing saves default to no residence; no financial conversion or residence migration is required. History records retain property identities, the player name and the simulation date.

No rent is earned from a residence. Existing holding costs (including simplified insurance and taxes), debt service, condition wear and repair costs continue. Repairs, preventive maintenance, replacement and cosmetic work remain available through Property workbench with the normal labor, qualification, time and material constraints. Renting, marketing for sale, changing occupancy and conversion require moving out first. Whole-building legacy renovations also require vacancy. Existing fictional tax calculations and financing rules are unchanged; this does not introduce real-world primary-residence tax treatment, owner living-expense savings, satisfaction bonuses or relocation logistics.

Validation executed: 62 tests passed in 29.91 seconds using test_personal_residence.py, test_property_categories.py, test_property_scale.py, test_campaign_product.py and test_saved_games.py. Coverage includes no rental income with continuing holding costs, successful and failed moves, tenant preservation, single-residence validation, blocked conflicting uses, resource-consuming repairs while occupied, persistence, seeded daily/reload equivalence and UI rendering. Two pre-existing dependency deprecation warnings remain. No live campaign was changed during QA.
