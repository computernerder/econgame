# Full-game implementation tracker

Authority: the comprehensive handoff and recorded user design decisions. Release 0.4 brings the interrupted expansion into the playable project; it does not declare all handoff milestones complete.

## Available in 0.4

Workforce policies/benefits, career actions, shared services, recruitment and manager authority; startups, phased openings, equipment expansion, integration and divestment; space leases and owner renovation; fictional finance/taxes, regions and civic projects; franchise contracts; actionable inbox, score history, separate campaign modes, optional succession and isolated forecasts all have commands and visible controls.

The inherited property/business tests and expansion/workforce tests pass. New product tests cover screen rendering, campaign safety, forecasts, leases and franchise accounting. Actual-save migration and one simulated year passed on a disposable copy. See VALIDATION.md.

## Still open

- Comprehensive T01–T18 acceptance/exploit coverage, including complex ownership transfers, franchise/default edge cases and policy/lease interactions.
- Decades-long balance and stability; 1,000/10,000-person benchmarks and resulting indexing/persistence work.
- Paged employee/search interfaces and large-hierarchy usability; current screens may become long at scale.
- Full headquarters relocation/closure flow, richer scheduling and secondary employment controls, and further coverage of detailed workforce requirements.
- Distribution packaging and clean-machine Windows/WebView2 qualification.
- More sophisticated profitability/culture attribution and seasonal balance. Regional economics and taxes remain a bounded fictional model.

Advanced manufacturing, public/minority equity, researched historical eras and multiplayer remain later work in the handoff.
