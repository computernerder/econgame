# Using logistics and ownership benefits

1. Buy a logistics company from the business market, or start one from Expansion.
2. Open Organization → Companies & real estate → Rearrange business ownership.
3. Move the logistics company under the business it should serve, or place both under the same holding company. Subsidiaries and sister companies in that corporate group share the benefit. Separate businesses owned directly by your personal portfolio do not automatically share transport.
4. Advance to a staffed working day. The business page’s Ownership benefits panel shows the carrier, deliveries, transport cost and savings.
5. To combine financing, acquire an operating bank. A bank can own the group; on the borrowing company’s Financing page, select that bank for preferential terms. Financing is a separate approved loan, not automatic borrowing.

## Capacity and cost

One local delivery needs 30 qualified driver minutes, 10 overlapping dispatcher minutes and one fleet slot. Each truck supports up to eight slots per day. Cross-region group deliveries use two slots. Leave, skills and shared assignments affect available staff capacity. Group clients use capacity first; outside customers can buy the remaining slots.

Group charges allocate payroll, premises and overhead (net of shared-staff reimbursements), plus $50 of running cost per slot. If group service is unavailable or more expensive, the operation uses an outside carrier at $300 per delivery. Actual unpaid internal charges remain debts owed to the logistics company. Internal charges cancel in consolidated profit; actual payroll, fuel and outside costs remain.

Delivery demand is rounded up from each day’s business volume: 100 retail items, grocery baskets or meals; 1,000 fuel gallons; 50 boutique items; or one vehicle per delivery. Trades, construction and factories need one material delivery per ten qualified project hours. These delivery costs are now explicit operating expenses.

Transport and bank financing can work together. Multiple carriers share finite demand; the same delivery cannot receive several discounts. Reorganizing or selling a company changes its eligibility for future transport, while existing loan contracts retain their terms.
