# Configurable revenue streams and equipment kits — 0.26.0

## Implemented behavior

Business pages now offer Revenue streams and equipment kits, with direct links from the revenue summary. Players can enable/disable offerings, set relative staff-time weights, set prices (50–175% of standard for new offerings; fuel retains 75–150%, with existing franchise bounds enforced), purchase capability kits and review specialty-specific project quotes. Existing companies retain their previous model until their first kit purchase or stream configuration. Existing accepted fees and earned work are preserved.

- Gas stations: fuel, convenience groceries, coffee and packaged snacks. Add-ons purchase and consume their own inventory, track delivery lots and spoilage (seven/three days), collect sales tax, and share cashier/attendant coverage with fuel. Core and add-on prices affect demand. Automatic stock purchases follow the existing stock policy and cumulative management purchasing authority; blocked orders enter the existing approval inbox and stop skips. Player kit purchases are not automatically delegated.
- Engineering: PCB design, support retainers, consulting, drawing review/stamping, embedded controls design and prototype testing. New project quotes vary deterministically by specialty and price. Automatic acceptance tries another enabled specialty when one lacks capacity or credentials, but an authority refusal still stops acceptance. Retainers/consulting and active projects share finite time. Stamping uses only available engineers with a current professional_engineer credential; existing credential courses remain the way to obtain/renew it. Staff leave, shared work and equipment condition reduce capacity. Accepted jobs retain their original fees and material budgets even when prices change or an offering is disabled. Completed jobs are invoiced once and collect after 14 days.
- Factories: existing general production plus electronics assembly, precision machined parts and packaged goods. Named equipment bundles are capitalized into the existing equipment ledger and depreciate through the existing finance rules. Setup takes days; tools/upkeep continue while a kit is disabled. Manufacturing uses shared operator/production-worker capacity, prepaid materials, condition-dependent defects, separate stream earnings, and collection 30 days after completion. Existing finished goods are shipped without buying their materials again.

Kits do not create employees, free cash, permanent income bonuses or duplicate productive hours. Prices influence demand, setup and staffing gate work, and unfunded materials stop production. Buying a kit must leave cash for 30 days of known obligations plus the new kit's upkeep. Accepted specialty material commitments appear in cash forecasts. Disabled offerings retain equipment and upkeep; active contracts continue. Repeated kit purchases are refused. Stream inventory participates in existing business asset liquidation and reconciles to the ledger.

## UI and persistence

Kit cards show equipment contents, price, setup time, recurring costs, staff/licenses, typical contract economics, material costs, demand, stock/shelf life, current work share and actual latest-day output/revenue. Funding and credential blockers link to their controls. Staffing guidance includes enabled gas-station offerings and the manufacturing labor recipe. Controls are collapsed to keep the operation page manageable; monetary displays stay in whole dollars.

State is saved in systems.revenue_kits, with per-business offers, dated stock lots and immutable material/stream attribution for accepted jobs. Existing employee identities and campaign saves are not rewritten during installation. Read-only views do not initialize the system or draw simulation randomness. The legacy day pipeline remains the path for unconfigured businesses.

## Limits and next step

This kit catalog covers the three requested examples: gas stations, engineering and manufacturing. Other industries retain their existing operating/service models. Kits share the location's equipment condition; there is no machine-by-machine condition, floor-space allocation, custom kit editor or individual kit resale. Factory order progress uses 60 order minutes per shipped product; at standard capacity that consumes 30 operator minutes and 15 production-worker minutes. Customer/project outcomes use the game's fictional economics, not real-world prices or licensing rules. Work shares reserve capacity; unused time is not automatically reassigned to another stream. Purchasing/planning forecasts intentionally treat uncertain future customer receipts conservatively.

Next increment: extend the same catalog/constraints to additional industries and add individual equipment replacement/resale and floor-space requirements. Restart the game after installing 0.26.0.
