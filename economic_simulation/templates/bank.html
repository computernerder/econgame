Bank operations

This bank serves simulated outside customers. Each staffed visit brings a $500 deposit and a $10 service fee. Customers request 1% of deposits back each weekday; deposits earn {{ b.deposit_rate_bps/100 }}% annually, credited daily.

Credit officers can originate up to two $20,000 loans per working day. Loans repay principal over 12 monthly installments and pay accrued interest. There is a 3% modeled chance of default at the first installment, which writes off the outstanding balance.

Unpaid withdrawals remain deposit liabilities and trigger a funding notice. Existing loans continue to settle when new lending is paused.
