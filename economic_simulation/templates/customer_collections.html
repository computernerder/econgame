Customer collections

Late payment is different from a default. A promised late payment retries automatically on its new date. You can request earlier payment or pursue recovery here; a headquarters is optional.

Managers own collections by default: reminders, installments, contingency agencies, justified legal recovery and small exhausted-balance write-offs within your limits. Routine invoices wait in the manager’s queue; only questions need your attention in Decision inbox. You can override an individual invoice below. Completed actions appear in Leadership activity.
