Rearrange business ownership

Move a business out of an unrelated company or group it under a different owner. Its subsidiaries, employees, real estate and debts move with the business. This is an internal transfer at book value, with no cash payment or sale.

Choose Personal portfolio to remove a subsidiary from a company while keeping it. You cannot move a company beneath itself or its subsidiaries. Existing guarantees and leases remain in force; inherited employment policies follow the new group.
