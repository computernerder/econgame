How this facility fills

Property managers need 30 qualified minutes per new tenant, up to three tenants per working day. Local demand caps new occupancy at 95%. About one in 40 tenants leaves at the beginning of each month. Existing tenants pay daily shares of their monthly rent, including weekends.

Maintenance needs two minutes per unit each weekday. Uncovered work costs $36 per hour in outsourced upkeep. The units are part of this facility's fit-out; its industrial building can be leased or company-owned.
